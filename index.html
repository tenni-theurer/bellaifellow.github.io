How judgment gets built

Judgment is built by grappling with real things, questioning them, and talking them through with others. Each path does that from a different angle. Pick one, or all three.

I · Practice

Homebrew AI Club

Advanced is for people who run agents, pipelines, or loops every day; demos go to full depth. Intermediate is for people who use AI daily and have gone past prompting.

II · Principles

AI and Civilization

A deep-reading seminar on the texts today's AI arguments came from: Descartes, Turing, Hobbes, Wiener, Schelling. Short readings, sent ahead. Two hours at the table working out what they say.

No lecture and no expert at the head of the table. A moderator keeps the argument honest; everyone has read the same pages. No technical background needed; the readings are the only prerequisite.

Not in San Francisco? The club's Intermediate cohort already meets by video. The seminar, the cases, and the Advanced club each open a Wednesday video cohort once six people ask for it on the application. The seminar and the cases share one evening slot; whichever fills first runs. Times are Pacific. Nobody pays until a cohort is confirmed.

Mixed cohorts, on purpose. Past cohorts have included lawyers, recruiters, executives, engineers, and students. What they had in common was the reason they came: to expand how they think, with the tools as the means. That is still the only entry requirement.

Our approach

Everything else about AI, you can rent. The models, the tools, the agents are the same for you as for everyone else, and they get better whether you do or not. Your judgment is the part that's yours.

There is no course for judgment. It gets built with experience and in company, which is why the method matters as much as the material.
