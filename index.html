How judgment gets built

Judgment is built by grappling with real things, questioning them, and talking them through with others. Each path does that from a different angle. Pick one, or all three.

I · Practice

Homebrew AI Club

A working session for people running real AI agents, tools, and loops. Members demo their setups live, say what worked and what broke, and we dig into it together.

Advanced is for people who run agents, pipelines, or loops every day; demos go to full depth. Intermediate is for people who want to do much more than simple prompts.

II · Principles

AI and Civilization

A deep-reading seminar on the texts today's AI arguments came from: Descartes, Turing, Hobbes, Wiener, Schelling. Short readings, sent ahead. Two hours at the table working out what they say.

No lecture and no expert at the head of the table. A moderator keeps the argument honest; everyone has read the same pages. No technical background needed; the readings are the only prerequisite.

Sundays 4–6pm PT
Six sessions, Sept 27 to Nov 1 · In person in SF

Not in San Francisco? Two cohorts meet by video: the club's Intermediate cohort on Thursdays and How Society Shapes Technology on Wednesday evenings. The Advanced club holds a few remote seats for experienced builders. Times are Pacific. Nobody pays until a cohort is confirmed.

Mixed cohorts, on purpose. Past cohorts have included engineers and product people, AI researchers, UI designers, founders, lawyers, recruiters, executives, and students. What they had in common was the reason they came: to expand how they think, with the tools as the means. That is still the only entry requirement.

Our approach

Everything else about AI, you can rent. The models, the tools, the agents are the same for you as for everyone else, and they get better whether you do or not. Your judgment is the part that's yours.

There is no course for judgment. It gets built with experience and in company, which is why the method matters as much as the material.
