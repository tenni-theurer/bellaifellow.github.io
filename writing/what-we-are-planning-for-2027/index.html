What we're planning for 2027

By Sunil Paul

The Bell AI Fellowship is an attempt to build a new kind of education for the age of AI.

We start from the core belief that as machines gain expertise and the ability to think, people must cultivate judgment, discernment and wisdom. Some people call this “taste.” We think these can be trained, in small cohorts, through shared work and argument.

In 2026 we piloted three ways of doing it:

  • Practice. Fellows built with AI, in community with other builders, and showed each other what worked and what broke, from agent harnesses to personal information systems.
  • Precedent. We learned and discussed how society shaped earlier technologies like nuclear power, chemicals, ridesharing and social media.
  • Principles. We read original authors, from Aristotle and Turing to Anthropic's constitution, and argued about them.

In 2027 we are expanding, guided by what builds judgment and what people tell us they most need to learn. Topics we're considering:

  • The original papers of AI
  • Data centers
  • The politics behind AI
  • AI and health
  • Writing and AI
  • Recursive self-improvement
  • Existential risk
  • Is AI a bubble?
  • Case studies of AI deployments
  • Mindfulness as a practice for judgment

We are bringing in outside instructors to lead them and adding cohorts in other locations, including Washington, DC.

We are looking for fellows, for people who want to teach, and for the topics we're missing. The working list of classes is on the What's next page, where you can also propose a class or offer to lead one.
