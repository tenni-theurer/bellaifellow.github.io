HAC. Homebrew AI Club

Judgment and taste can't be taught. They have to be caught.

A show-and-tell for people actually running AI agents, tools, and loops — where you catch judgment by watching real people run real AI, and build taste by showing yours.

what is HAC?

Three people demo their real setup, live: the agent, the pipeline, the prompt stack, the workflow they built for themselves. Then everyone names one thing that broke this month. Then we argue about it.

You don't get judgment from a tutorial. You get it from watching people try, succeed, fail — and discussing it in real time. No slides. No pitch decks. Just what's on your machine.

It's the Homebrew Computer Club, updated: a room of people with different skills, tinkering together, comparing notes on what actually works — and building taste for what's real versus what just sounds good.

what it isn't

  • Not a course. No syllabus, no sequence, nothing to catch up on.
  • Not a lecture. Nobody stands at the front and explains AI to you.
  • Not for beginners. Plenty of tutorials exist for that. Be ready to build.
