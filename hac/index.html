HAC. Homebrew AI Club

Judgment and Taste. Can't be TAUGHT. Must be CAUGHT. — How? From watching real people run real AI.

what is HAC?

A show-and-tell for people actually running AI agents, tools, and loops — where you build judgment by watching how other people use them, and develop taste by getting feedback from others.

You don't get judgment and taste from a tutorial. You get it from watching people trying, succeeding, failing, and most importantly, discussing in real time — and from doing the same with yours. Three people demo their real setup, live: the agent, the pipeline, the prompt stack, the workflow they built for themselves. Then everyone names one thing that broke or disappointed them this month. No slides. No pitch decks. Just what's on your machine.

It's the Homebrew Computer Club, updated: a room of people with different skills, tinkering together, comparing notes on what actually works — and building taste for what's real versus what just sounds good.

what it isn't

  • No, it's not a course. No syllabus, no sequence, nothing to catch up on.
  • No, there's no lecture. Nobody stands at the front and explains AI to you.
  • No, this isn't for beginners. Plenty of tutorials exist for that. Be ready to build.
