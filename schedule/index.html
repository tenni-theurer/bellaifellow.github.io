Applying

Each path takes applications separately. These are applications, not sign-ups — each one is read, and places are offered by what makes a strong room rather than by who got there first. Five minutes each, and they close September 22. Tuition is $180 a path.

Homebrew AI Club — build judgment through demos.
AI and Civilization — think through the original texts.
How Society Shapes Technology — understand the historical record.

If a fee would keep you out, say so in your application. We set places aside for exactly that.
