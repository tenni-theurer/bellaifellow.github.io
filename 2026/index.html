What we did in 2026

We start from the belief that as machines gain expertise and the ability to think, people must cultivate judgment, discernment and wisdom. In 2026 we piloted three ways of training them, in small cohorts, through shared work and argument.

Three paths

Practice

Homebrew AI Club

Fellows built with AI and showed each other what worked and what broke, from agent harnesses to personal information systems. No instructor and no curriculum: members demo their real setups, and the group digs in.

Every other Thursday, October to December · In person in SF and by video · See the club →

Principles

AI and Civilization

A deep-reading seminar on the texts today's AI arguments came from, from Aristotle and Turing to Anthropic's constitution. Short readings sent ahead, then two hours working out what they say.

Sundays, September 27 to November 1 · In person in SF · See the seminar →

Precedent

How Society Shapes Technology

Cases from 120 years of technologies being sped up, redirected, or stopped: nuclear power, chemicals, ridesharing, social media. Then the question of what that record says about AI.

Wednesdays, September 30 to November 4 · By video · See the cases →

Dates and sessions for all three are on the fall 2026 schedule.

The idea behind it

“Taste” is the word people use for what machines still need from us. We think the better words are judgment, discernment and wisdom, and that all three can be trained.
