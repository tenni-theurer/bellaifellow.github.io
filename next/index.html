How this works

Leading a class

Offering to teach works the same way as proposing a topic: fill in the same short form and say you'd lead it.

You can lead one of the topics above, a new section of a returning class, or a subject of your own. We look for people with real depth in a field who would rather run a good conversation than give a lecture. We handle recruiting, admissions, payment and logistics. You bring the subject and run the sessions.

What we provide

  • Motivated, intelligent, interesting students
  • Recruiting, through our mailing list, past cohorts, and the founders' networks
  • Logistics: admissions, tuition, scheduling, recordings, a video setup, and space in San Francisco and, tentatively, Washington, DC
  • Help designing sessions, from people who have run three seasons of them
  • Training on teaching lessons that build judgment and taste

What we ask

  • Lead every session, or arrange a co-leader
  • Send readings or prep at least a week ahead
  • Keep each session discussion-led; a short briefing to set up the material is fine
  • Read the feedback with us afterward
Commitment
Four to six sessions, 1.5–2 hours each, plus prep
Format
In person in SF, by video, or hybrid
