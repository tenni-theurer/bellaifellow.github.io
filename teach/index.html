Call for instructors · Next season

Lead a class with us

Bring a subject you know deeply.

We're looking for people to lead classes next season, either a cohort of one of our three paths or a class of your own design. We handle recruiting, admissions, payment, and logistics. You bring the subject and run the sessions.

Propose a class →

Who we're looking for

People with real depth in a field who would rather run a good conversation than give a lecture.

  • Practitioners: engineers, founders, and operators who build with AI and can show their work
  • Scholars and writers who know a body of texts or history well enough to let others argue with it
  • People who have run seminars, workshops, design reviews, or reading groups, and liked doing it
  • Anyone who can hold a mixed group of engineers, lawyers, designers, and students to a high standard

Our method is the same across every path: real material, everyone prepared, and the group doing the thinking. The leader sets the question and keeps the argument honest.

Two ways to teach

1
Lead a cohort of an existing path
The curriculum, readings, and session designs already exist and have been run. We need leaders for added sections: for example, an in-person How Society Shapes Technology section, a video AI and Civilization section, or the Homebrew AI Club lunch cohort.
2
Propose a class of your own
Four to six sessions on a question with depth, one that builds judgment about AI rather than skill with a single tool. Proposals that fit go on the candidate list, and they run the same way the others do: when enough people want them.

What we provide

  • Recruiting, through our mailing list, past cohorts, and the founders' networks
  • Applications, admissions, and tuition collection
  • A teaching assistant for scheduling, reminders, recordings, and participant email
  • Help designing sessions, from people who have run three seasons of them
  • A video setup, and space in San Francisco for in-person sections
  • A post-season survey, so you know how it landed

What we ask

  • Lead every session of the season, or arrange a co-leader
  • Send readings or prep at least a week ahead
  • Keep each session discussion-led; a short briefing to set up the material is fine
  • Read the post-season feedback with us

Propose a class

A short form. No syllabus needed yet.

  • The question your class would work on, in a sentence or two
  • Who it's for
  • Format, length, and when you could run it
  • Your background, and a link to something you've written, built, or taught

We read every proposal and reply to each one. If it's a fit, we'll set up a call and work out the design together.
